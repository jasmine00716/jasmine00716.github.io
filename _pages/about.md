I am a medical AI researcher working at the intersection of **multimodal learning, foundation models, and medical image analysis**. My research focuses on building clinically meaningful AI systems that can learn across institutions, imaging modalities, and downstream tasks.

I received my M.S. in Computer Engineering from **Korea University**, where I worked with Prof. Jin Tae Kwak on deep learning for acute ischemic stroke imaging, after earning a B.S. in Computer Science from **Dongduk Women's University**. My graduate research led to **3D-MoReT**, a lightweight 3D CNN–Transformer model that generates multiphase collateral maps from 4D MR perfusion imaging.

From September 2024 to June 2026, I worked at **DeepClue Inc.**, where I led the Engineering & Manufacturing Team. I translated collateral-imaging research into MRI and CT software as a medical device, covering clinical requirements, system architecture, implementation, verification, regulatory documentation, and hospital deployment.

Research Interests
======

- Large-scale multimodal and foundation models for medical AI
- Vision–language learning and clinical representation learning
- Medical image analysis across MR, CT, and multimodal clinical data
- Efficient 3D deep learning for classification, regression, and segmentation
- Translational AI and software as a medical device

Selected Highlights
======

- First author of **3D Mobile Regression Vision Transformer for Collateral Imaging in Acute Ischemic Stroke**, published in the *International Journal of Computer Assisted Radiology and Surgery*.
- Co-inventor on a Korean patent application for generating hypoperfusion lesion maps and predicting ischemic penumbra.
- Contributed to the **ISLES 2024** multimodal infarct-segmentation challenge team, which placed ninth.
- Received the **Best Poster Award** at the 38th Annual Conference of the Korean Society of Medical Imaging Informatics and an **Honorable Mention** in Samsung AI Challenge 2023.
