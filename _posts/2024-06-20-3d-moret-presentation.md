I presented my first-author work, **3D Mobile Regression Vision Transformer for Collateral Imaging in Acute Ischemic Stroke**, as a poster and a three-minute oral presentation at CARS 2024.

In this study, I developed **3D-MoReT**, a lightweight 3D CNN–Transformer regression model that generates five-phase collateral maps directly from 4D dynamic susceptibility contrast MR perfusion images. The model was evaluated using data from 952 patients across two university hospitals and achieved performance comparable to substantially larger baseline models.

Presenting the project provided an opportunity to discuss efficient deep-learning architectures and clinically useful collateral imaging with researchers working across medical imaging and computer-assisted intervention.
