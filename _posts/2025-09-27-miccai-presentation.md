I presented **PathRadX** at the MedAGI 2025 workshop, held in conjunction with the 28th International Conference on Medical Image Computing and Computer Assisted Intervention (MICCAI 2025).

Our study, *From Pathology to Radiology: Evaluating the Applicability of Pathology Foundation Models*, explored whether representations learned from histopathology could transfer to radiology classification tasks without end-to-end fine-tuning. We evaluated modality-adaptation and classification strategies across the MURA, RSNA Pneumonia, and MedFMC datasets.

The conference was a valuable opportunity to share our work and learn about recent advances in medical image analysis and foundation models.
