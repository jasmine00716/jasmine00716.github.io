## Overview

This project explored final-infarct prediction using multimodal acute stroke imaging and clinical context. The proposed method used a 3D Vision Transformer with NCCT, CTP, CTA, and Tmax inputs.

## My Contributions

- Contributed to problem definition and multimodal data preparation.
- Participated in method and experimental design.
- Performed comparative analysis and supported abstract preparation.
- Collaborated with a teammate who implemented the code and executed the experiments.

## Outcome

The team placed ninth in the ISLES 2024 Challenge. The submitted method was subsequently included in the challenge consortium's revised preprint.
