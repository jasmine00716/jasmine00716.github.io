## Overview

This study benchmarked CNN- and Transformer-based architectures for 3D ischemic stroke lesion segmentation using diffusion-weighted MR imaging.

## Study Design

- Compared 3D U-Net, 3D U-Net++, 3D MobileViT, and 3D SwinUNETR architectures.
- Evaluated single-modality DWI against multimodal DWI and ADC inputs.
- Analyzed performance across 651 stroke patient cases and different lesion sizes.

## Outcome

Multimodal DWI and ADC input consistently outperformed DWI-only training across the evaluated architectures. The results were presented at the 2024 IEIE Annual Conference.
