## Overview

PathRadX investigates whether representations learned from histopathology can generalize to grayscale radiology images. The study combines input-level modality adaptation with image- and study-level classification strategies to test cross-modal transfer without end-to-end fine-tuning of the foundation model.

## My Contributions

- Prepared and organized data used in the evaluation pipeline.
- Supported quantitative result analysis and comparison across datasets and strategies.
- Contributed to manuscript drafting and interpretation of the experimental results.

The collaborating researchers led the model design, implementation, and experiment execution.

## Evaluation Scope

Experiments covered the MURA, RSNA Pneumonia, and MedFMC datasets, with comparisons across channel manipulation, pseudo-coloring, single-instance learning, and multiple-instance learning.
